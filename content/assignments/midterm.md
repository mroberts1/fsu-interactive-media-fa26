### I: Key Concepts

(3 x 20%)

Write a short entry up to 250 words in length - about a page double-spaced - explaining **three** of the following terms or concepts. In each case, identify  their source or origin, what they refer to, and their  significance in relation to the history of interactive media systems.

- Virtual community
- Graceful degradation
- Garden of Forking Paths
- Metalepsis
- Hyperfiction
- Turing Test

### II: Interactive Environments

 (1 x 40%)

Choose any **two** of the interactive environments listed below and compare and contrast them, using the following questions as a guide (you don't have to answer all of them).

- What exactly *was* the game/interactive system in question? What kind of world was it?

- What was its historical context?  What was innovative about it in its time? 

- What could you do in this environment that was new, different, or unusual?

- How was it a continuation of and how did extend it? What subsequent developments did it anticipate?

- What were the main similarities and differences between the games/immersive systems that you are comparing?

Choose any two:

- *ELIZA*
- *Adventure*
- *Zork*
- *LambdaMOO*
- *Habitat*
- *Myst*
- *Habbo Hotel*
- *Second Life*
- *inZOI*
- *ChatGPT*

***
 
**Style**

Your answer should be written in college essay style, using course reading assignments as your stylistic model: avoid conversational style, slang words, informal use of "you" instead of "the player/user/viewer", personal narratives and/or references); care with spelling and punctuation; expository structure (paragraphs); focused discussion avoiding digressions).

Write full sentences: do not give bullet-point or note-format answers.

**Approach**

One of the main goals of this assignment is to demonstrate close attention to source texts and/or audiovisual media assigned to date, as well as a strong grasp of relevant concepts and/or key points that they make. You are therefore encouraged to reference sources and their authors by name and (where relevant) date of the source in question.

**Sources and Citations**

Full academic citations and a bibliography are **not** required in this assignment.

If you reference an online source, don't just post a URL: create a link either in-text or in a footnote or list of sources at the end of the document.

**Format**

- No cover page necessary
- Header with your name, course name and number, instructor name, date
- Short title indicating which games/systems you are discussing
- Number pages
- DOUBLE-spacing
- MS Word document format: .doc, .docx (not PDF)
- Standard 12-pt font (Arial, Helvetica, Times New Roman, Georgia, Palatino, etc.)

**Submission**

Please upload your answers as a single document to the Blackboard Assignment linked in the sidebar for this purpose.
