title: Assignments and Evaluation
---

- **Commentary** (Blackboard or Canvas, weekly, 10 required: 100-250-word response to reading/screening assignments): 30%

- **Midterm: Game Report/Analysis** 

- **Special Interest Group (SIG) Paper** (open topic) (1,000 words, 4 pages double-spaced, not including bibliography, letter grade): 15%

- **Special Interest Group (SIG) Presentation** (in-class, slides optional but not required): 15%

- **Final: Game Report/Analysis**: (1,000 words / 4 pages double-spaced): 20%
